# 83: Iron Curtain Crisis
**A Cold War strategy simulation game where your decisions determine global survival.**
---
## **🌍 Setting**
- **Time:** November 7, 1983, 03:00 AM Moscow Time.
- **Role:** Marshal Nikolai Ogarkov, Chief of the Soviet General Staff.
- **Crisis:** NATO’s **Able Archer 83** exercise is underway—is it a drill or a first-strike cover?
---
## **🎮 Gameplay**
- **Manage real-time events** (NATO movements, KGB reports, technical alerts).
- **Choose from 12 actions** (DEFCON 1, intel probes, diplomacy, sabotage, etc.).
- **NATO responds dynamically** to your decisions.
- **Stats affected:** Military readiness, trust, intel, paranoia, crisis level.
---
## **🏆 Endings**
1. **💀 Nuclear War** (Crisis level ≥ 95%)
2. **🕊️ Diplomatic Victory** (High trust, low crisis)
3. **💥 NATO First Strike** (Low readiness, high crisis)
---
## **🎭 Themes**
- **Paranoia & misinformation** in the Cold War.
- **High-stakes decisions** with global consequences.
- **Historical accuracy** (Able Archer 83, Reagan, Soviet doctrine).
---
